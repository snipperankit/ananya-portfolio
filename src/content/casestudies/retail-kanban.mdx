## The situation

The team handled both new features and a constant stream of urgent support, and everything
felt equally on fire. Nothing was visible, so nothing was predictable.

## What we changed

We put every item on a shared board, set explicit work-in-progress limits, and agreed a
simple policy for how urgent work entered the flow.

## The result

Interruptions stopped derailing planned work, and lead time dropped sharply within six weeks.
