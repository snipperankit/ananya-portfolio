      *This case study is based on a challenge encountered and redesigned as a Simulation to reflect real-world complexities, here for a Solution Train of a Radar project in the Automotive Industry. The diagnostics, interventions, and structural outcomes combine direct practical experience with established industry frameworks to demonstrate structured problem-solving methodologies. All the diagrams are generated using my own data and not plagiarised.*


      **📌 Primary Skill**


      Facilitation as a Release Train Engineer, Agile Governance, Delivery Management, ASPICE SUP.9 (Problem Resolution Management)


      **🌐 Project Context and Team Setup**


      - The overall project team consists of a large-scale Solution Train split across two distinct Agile Release Trains (ARTs) along with some supporting functional teams.
          
          ART 1 → 8 Vehicle Integration core development teams as well as SW Testing teams
          
          ART 2 → 7 Algorithm Development functional teams, Vehicle Testing and HiL teams
          
          Other supporting teams → Eg. Governance-based teams, Supplier Monitoring teams etc




          
      - **Methodology**: SAFe framework with a hybrid Scrum/Scrumban approach at the team level

      - **Delivery Cadence**: Regular Customer delivery milestones are governed by the gate release process. The project is in an active build phase with future releases actively planned. Backlog planning is done with PI planning.

      **⛔️ Problem Statement**

       The collective Problem reports (PRs) across all ART Backlogs climbed to an unimaginable 500+ tickets. This backlog is a mixture of:

      - Customer Reported problem reports

      - Internal software defects

      - Quality gaps/Optimisation topics

      - Architectural technical debts


      Despite extensive tracking mechanisms, the net inflow of tickets consistently outpaced the outflow. An upcoming ASPICE SUP.9 (Problem Resolution Management) Audit has added to the pressure and requires brainstorming and a sustainable burn-down strategy that will not derail the upcoming feature release timelines. As a Release Train Engineer and Problem Resolution Manager, it is expected to lead this brainstorming strategy and track the execution until completion.


      Duration Constraint: 4 weeks 


      **⚠️ Pre-Requisites/ Assumptions/ Current Baseline**


      A Problem Report (PR) is a bug, a deviation, or a finding from a specification discovered across any stage of the SDLC lifecycle. In Jira, this is a ticket type that has a workflow from discovery to verification of the issue.


      As per SUP.9 guidelines, foundational processes are already active in the project:


      - A well documented and propagated Problem Report Burndown process

      - Weekly Burndown tracking meetings and reporting dashboards

      - A strict prioritisation based on technical and business impact
